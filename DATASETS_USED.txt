=== External Datasets Used ===

1. ISIC 2019 Classification Dataset
   Source: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/#2019
   Images: 25,331
   Classes: 8 (MEL, NV, BCC, AK, BKL, DF, VASC, SCC)
   License: CC-BY-NC 4.0
   Used for: Intermediate fine-tuning of classification models

2. ISIC 2018 Task 1 Segmentation Dataset
   Source: https://challenge.isic-archive.com/data/#2018
   Images: 2,594 with binary segmentation masks
   License: CC-BY-NC 4.0
   Used for: Pretraining segmentation encoder

3. Hackathon Training Data
   Classification: 11,411 images, 12 classes
   Segmentation: 1,800 train + 400 validation with masks
